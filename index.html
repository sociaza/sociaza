About Sociaza

Software made for focus, not distraction

Social publishing tools have become increasingly complex. What should be a straightforward workflow has been weighed down with enterprise feature sprawl, costly recurring subscriptions, invasive cloud tracking, and cluttered dashboards.

Sociaza is being created with a different philosophy: software should be quiet, reliable, and respect the creator's time and attention. By concentrating strictly on what matters—organizing your visual assets, planning your calendar, and crafting platform-ready posts—Sociaza provides a calm environment to manage your social presence.

Local-first and private by default
Your shop catalog, images, and OAuth credentials are stored on your local drive in SQLite. We do not maintain cloud servers that inspect, harvest, or aggregate your commercial data.
Restrained by design
No artificial engagement metrics, gamified badges, or notification noise. Just a fast, responsive interface tailored for doing actual work.
Strict API compliance
We integrate solely through official developer APIs with the minimal permissions necessary to perform publishing tasks.
